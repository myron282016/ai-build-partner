WHY AN AI BUILD PARTNER?

AI can build fast. But speed is not the same as knowing what to build.

AI tools can generate code, screens, automations, and entire features in minutes. The harder part is knowing what architecture to choose, what the AI got wrong, how the pieces should connect, what is safe to ship, and how to recover when something breaks.

That’s where I come in.

I combine 12+ years of software engineering experience with AI’s speed. We use AI to move faster, while I provide the technical direction: breaking the problem down, choosing the right approach, reviewing what AI produces, debugging issues, and helping you understand the system well enough to keep moving.

You are not paying me to type prompts into an AI tool. You are paying for an experienced technical partner who helps turn a business idea or client requirement into a working solution — faster with AI, but with the engineering judgment needed to build it correctly and confidently.
